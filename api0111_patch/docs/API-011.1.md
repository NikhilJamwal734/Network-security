# API-011.1 — Correlation Accuracy and False-Positive Triage

## Scope

`POST /api/v1/analyze/correlation-accuracy` invokes 11.0 on the original upload and
then adds `correlation_accuracy_review`. It does not re-read the uploaded file.
Existing normalized fields, stage reviews, scores, severities and findings are
preserved exactly; the new output is only an advisory sidecar.

## Conservative verification

* Revalidate each 11.0 candidate link against a bounded, platform-applicable,
  exact source locator: identical upload line, identifier and title across at
  least two *different* applicable review sections.
* Classify into `plausible_metadata_overlap`, `unsupported_metadata_link`, or
  `context_incomplete`. Metadata matches are not semantic identity.
* Flag repeated normalized finding metadata as **potential** overlap, and
  conflicting reported severities as quality observations. Neither finding
  is removed or changed.
* Abstain on missing, limited or truncated context. Explicit caps protect
  against excessive report sizes.

## False-positive interpretation

Rejected links are **not** "false-positive security findings". Accurate false
positive rates require ground truth and validation against deployment/network
behavior. No inference of actual enforcement, active IDS behavior, reachability,
exploitability, or safe remediation is made.

## Presentation

Dashboard: four counts, two short classifications at most, no detailed
observations. PDF: all inspected link decisions, source line, provenance,
justification, recommended analyst checks, metadata issues, limitations.

## Limits

At most 600 normalized findings, 160 link records, 500 source entries per
review section, 80 rendered decisions and 80 metadata observations. Review
is incomplete if caps or upstream incompleteness are detected.
